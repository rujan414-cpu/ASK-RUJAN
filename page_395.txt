4. Acid base reaction

The reaction in which acid and base reacts to form salt and water
is called acid base reaction. It is also a type of double displacement
reaction. In the acid base reaction, both the properties of acid and
base are lost by each other and form neutral compounds. So, it is also

known as a neutralization reaction.

Acid + Base _ Salt + Water

HCl (acid) + NaOH (base) — NaCl (base) + H,O (water
H,SO, (acid) + 2NaOH (base)— Na, SO, (base) + H,O (water)
KOH (base) + HNO, (acid) -> KNO, (salt) + H,O (water)
