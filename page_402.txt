Properties of carbon dioxide gas

Physical properties

a. It is an acidic or non-metallic oxide which is colourless and
odourless.

b. This gas dissolves in water to form carbonic acid, so it is sour in taste.

c.  Itis slightly soluble in water.

d. It is 1.5 times heavier than air.

e. It shows acidic properties and changes moist blue litmus paper red.

f. This gas is nontoxic, however, organisms die in the environment

of carbon dioxide by suffocation due to lack of oxygen gas.
g. Itcanbechanged to liquid at high pressure and low temperature.

h. When Carbon dioxide is cooled below -78°c, it changes to solid
form which is known as dry ice.

i. This gas is neither combustible nor it supports combustion.
Chemical properties

1. Carbon dioxide is neither combustible nor a supporter of
combustion.
