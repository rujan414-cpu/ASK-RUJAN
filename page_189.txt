Activity 7.6 Making a model of a parachute

Take scissors, a thin plastic sheet and thread. Stretch the plastic in
different steps and cut it into a circular shape as shown in Figure 7.16.
Tie equal pieces of thread at equal distances around the edges of the
circular plastic. Tie the open section of the threads in a single knot
and attach a toy or stone to it. Drop the prepared parachute model
from a height. Observe its fall. Does the parachute fall at a high speed
at the beginning and with a uniform speed at the last?
