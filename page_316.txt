Electromagnetic induction

Around the beginning of the nineteenth century (1800 AD), the vol-
taic cell was used as the main source of current. In the year 1831,
Michael Faraday discovered that when the magnetic force lines are
cut perpendicularly by a conducting wire, a voltage is generated in the
conducting wire and an electric current flows when the two ends of
the wire are connected in a circuit. This discovery brought a remark-
able change in the field of the source of electricity.
