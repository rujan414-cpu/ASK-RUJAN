In a hydraulic jack, when the releasing valve is tightened and the
lever is pushed down, the upper valve closes and the lower valve
opens. As a result, the force exerted on the pumping piston of the jack
increases and the load on the lifting piston is pushed a little higher.
After that, when the lever is pulled up, the bottom valve closes due
to high pressure and the top valve opens. As a result, the oil from
the reservoir fills the cylinder of the pumping piston. Then, in the
next stage of pumping, the load is pushed up again. By repeatedly
pushing the lever up and down, the load can be pushed up to the
required height.
