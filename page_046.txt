Phylum Chordata is divided into four sub-phyla. They are
Hemichordata, Urochordata, Cephalochordata, and Vertebrata.
The first three subphyla arecollectively called Protochordata
since animals belonging to these subphyla are more primitive
than the animals in Vertebrata. Here, we are going to discuss
sub-phylum Vertebrata only.
