Their body is elongated, flat, and streamlined. Their bodies
are covered by scales.

Their body is divisible into head, trunk, and tail. The neck
is absent.

Gills are found on the lateral side of head. They help in
respiration.

They have paired and unpaired fins for locomotion.
They have air sacs in their body.

Their heart is two-chambered.

These animals are poikilothermic.

They are unisexual animals.
They are mostly oviparous but few are viviparous.

Most fishes perform external fertilization but internal
fertilization is found in some fishes like sharks.

They live in water. All fishes, sea horse, etc.belong tothis
class.
