Unsaturated hydrocarbon

The hydrocarbons having double or triple covalent bond between
the carbon atoms are called unsaturated hydrocarbons. They are less
stable and are very reactive so they are known as olefins.They are
also known as alkene and alkyne.

Alkene

The hydrocarbons having a double covalent bond between the carbon
atoms are called alkene. Example ethene, propene, butene, etc. The
general formula of these compounds is CH, , where ‘n’ is the number
of carbon atoms. 7
