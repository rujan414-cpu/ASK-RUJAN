Figure 8.19 The upward force on the empty bottle when it is immersed in water

It is difficult to push the bottle into the water. It means the water is
pushing the bottle upward. As the bottle is pushed into the water, the
upward force exerted by the water goes on increasing until the bottle
is completely submerged. When the bottle is released, it returns to the
surface of the water. The net upward force by which water pushes
the object in it is called upthrust. All fluids exert upthrust but the
magnitude of the upthrust depends upon their density.
