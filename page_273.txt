Activity 10.10 Making a hand Jens

As shown in figure 10.48, take a plastic bottle, scissors, a hot glue
gun, a syringe, and water. Cut the plastic bottle into circular pieces.
Overlap the two pieces in such a way that the raised portions lie
outside, and stick them together with a hot glue gun. Then fill it with
water with the help of a syringe. Can you use the resulting lens to
read small letters in a book or magnify small objects?
