Activity 13.1: Observation of Digital Signal Graph

As shown in Fig. 13.6, construct an electric circuit by connecting
a 5 V alternating current source, a bulb, a switch, and conducting
wire. If the potential difference between the two ends of the bulb is
5 V, while turning the switch on, then this voltage is considered as a
high signal (1). When the switch is turned off, the potential difference
between the two ends of the bulb becomes zero. This is considered
as a low signal (0). Note the high signals and low signals generated in
the circuit as time passes and record them in a table and draw graphs
similar to the one shown in Figure 13.6.
