Results of Mendel’s experiment
Tall plants were produced from pure tall plants.

b. Tall and dwarf plants were produced in a ratio 3:1 from hybrid
tall plants.

c. Dwarf plants were produced from pure dwarf plants.
Phenotypic ratio and genotypic ratios in Mendel’s experiment

Phenotypic characters are the character seen externally in an organism.
Similarly, Genetic constitution or genetic makeup of an organism is
called genotypic character.

Phenotypic ratio is Tall: dwarf = 3:1
Genotypic ratio is pure tall: hybrid tall: pure dwarf = 1:2:1
