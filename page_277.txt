The ray diagrams of images formed by lenses depend on their shape.

When light rays are converged by a convex lens and diverged by a

concave lens, the following rules apply while drawing the ray diagrams:

(a) Light rays passing through the optical center of the lens do not
bend. They continue to move forward in a straight line.

(b) light rays travelling parallel to the principal axis converge and

pass through the focus. When the same rays pass through a
concave lens, they appear to diverge from the focus.
