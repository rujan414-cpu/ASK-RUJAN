Glycerol

The compound formed by replacing three hydrogen atoms of propane
with three hydroxyl groups is called glycerol. Glycerol is a trihydric
alcohol which is also known as glycerine. Its name is derived from a
Greek word glycerol which means sweet. It is a colourless and sweet
viscous liquid. It is soluble in water and alcohol but insoluble in
ether. Its IUPAC name is propane-1, 2, 3- triol. Its molecular formula
is C,H,(OH), and its structural formula is
