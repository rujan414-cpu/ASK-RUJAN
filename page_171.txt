Gravitation and Newton's universal law of gravitation

When Sir Isaac Newton, a British mathematician/physicist, saw a fruit

falling from a tree to the ground, he began to
wonder why the fruit did not fall horizontally
but only vertically. After doing much study
and thinking, he concluded that the attraction
between the apple and the earth caused the
fruit to fall towards the centerof the earth.
Similarly, he wondered how the planets, moon,
sun, stars, etc. are stuck in the sky.
