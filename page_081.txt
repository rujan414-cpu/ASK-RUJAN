This type of cell division is only confined to the mother cells of major
reproductive organs, i.e., the testis and ovary. In this cell division,
one diploid mother cell divides to form four haploid daughter cells
or gametes. A haploid cell is a cell in which the chromosome number
is reduced to half that of the mother cell. In meiosis, there is a slight
variation in the genetic makeup of the daughter cells produced from
the mother cell. This cell division completes in two phases.
