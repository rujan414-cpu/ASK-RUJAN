Reptilia

Snake Lizard Crocodile Tortoise
Figure 2.24

Animals such as snake, lizard, crocodile, tortoise, etc. crawl on
the ground. So they are grouped in the class reptilia. Animals in
this class have following characteristics:

a. Their bodies are covered with dry and horny scales.

b. Their bodiesare divided into four parts: head, neck, trunk,
and tail.

c. They have two pairs of limbs which crawl on the ground for
locomotion.

d. These animals are poikilothermic.
