Following measures can be adopted for the proper management
of industrial chemicals like cement, glass, ceramics, plastic, fiber,
chemical fertilizers, soap, detergents, pesticides, etc.

Glass, ceramics, plastic and fiber pieces should not be thrown
haphazardly and should be managed in proper places.

Cement sacs should be tied and covered properly to prevent the
dust blow.

Organic or compost fertilizer should be used instead of the
chemical fertilizers.

Chemical fertilizers and pesticides should be used only as
suggested by the agriculture expert.

The waste water after the use of soap and detergent should not be
directly mixed to water sources or agricultural land but should
be collected in separate places.
