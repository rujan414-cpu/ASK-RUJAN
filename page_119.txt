white blood cells ranges from about 4 thousand to 11 thousand per
cubic millimetre of blood. They are formed in bone marrow and are
destroyed in the spleen. When there is an abnormal increase in the
number of white blood cells in the blood, then they destroy other
blood cells, resulting in a disease called leukemia or blood cancer.

White blood cells fight against disease-causing germs that enter the
body from outside and destroy them. So, these cells are referred to as
the soldiers of the human body. These cells increase the immunity
of our body. An abnormal decrease of white blood cells leads to a
disease called leukopenia.

c. Platelet

cells in the human body. They are microscopic blood cells. Per cubic
millimetre of blood contains about 2 to 4 lakhs platelets.

2 to 3 days, and they are also destroyed in
