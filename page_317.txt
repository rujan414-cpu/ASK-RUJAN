Faraday's law of electromagnetic induction

The voltage produced in a coil depends on the strength of the mag-
netic field (electromotive force) and the number of turns in the coil.
The greater the strength of the magnetic field and the number of turns
of the coil. the more the voltage produced. The magnitude of the re-
sulting voltage also depends on how quickly or slowly the magnetic
field lines link the coil. On moving the magnet in and out of the coil
at a very slow speed, negligible voltage is induced in the coil, but
on moving the magnet in and out at a higher speed, more voltage is
induced in the coil.
