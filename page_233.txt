In winter in very cold places, as the temperature of the environment
gradually decreases, the volume of water inside the pipe increases
and it exerts pressure on the inner wall of the pipe. When the water
freezes and becomes ice, the pressure can become very high and the
pipe may burst. For the same reason, a bottle filled with water that is
kept in the deep freeze cracks.
