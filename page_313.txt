The direction of the magnetic field formed around the current flow-
ing solenoid can be found by using Maxwell's right-hand grip rule.
According to this rule, if a solenoid is held in the right hand such
that the fingers indicate the direction of the current through the so-
Jenoid, the thumb points to the north pole of the magnetic field de-
veloped in the solenoid. The strength of the magnetic field around a
solenoid depends on the following factors.

(a) Magnitude of the current in the solenoid
(b) Number of turns in the coil of the solenoid

(e) A material placed inside the solenoid (core). Material such as a
soft iron cylinder increases the strength of the magnetic field.

Since the magnetic field created by the solenoid is temporary, it is
used to make an electromagnet.
