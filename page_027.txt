Plants such as Cycas, Juniper, Pinus etc. are kept under flowering
plants but they bear cones instead of flowers. They have naked
seeds without fruits. So these plants are kept in the sub-division
gymnosperm. Following are the characteristics of the plants
under the sub-division gymnosperm:

a. Incourse of evolution of plants, gymnosperms are the first
plant to produce seed.

b. They bear cones instead of flowers. Male and female cones
are separate. So they are unisexual. Pollination takes place
through wind.

There is no ovary in cone and hence no fruits. Seed is naked.
Their leaves are elongated and needle like.
Stem is enclosed within thick bark.

Shape of these plants is inverted cone like and hence they
are called coniferous plants.

g. The roots of these plants are spread far into the ground.
