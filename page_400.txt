Laboratory preparation of carbon dioxide gas

In laboratory carbon dioxide gas is prepared by the chemical reaction

of limestone (CaCO,) with dilute hydrochloric acid (dil. HCl).

Calcium Carbonate + dilute Hydrochloric acid > Calcium chloride
+ water + Carbon dioxide

CaCO,(s) + 2HCl(aq) — CaCl, (aq)+ H,O (1) + CO,(g)
Apparatus required

Wolfe's bottle, gas jar, thistle funnel, delivery tube, rubber cork, and
some test tubes.

Chemicals required

Limestone or marble pieces or calcium carbonate powder or egg
shells, dilute hydrochloric acid. phenolphthalein, lime water, sodium
hydroxide solution, blue litmus paper.

Method/Procedure

Practical activity: 1

a. Collect the apparatus and chemicals required for the preparation
of gas.

b. Keep some pieces of limestone or marble pieces or calcium
carbonate powder or egg shells into the Woulfe'’s bottle.
