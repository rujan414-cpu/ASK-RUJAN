Saturated hydrocarbon

The hydrocarbons having a single covalent bond between the carbon
atoms are called saturated hydrocarbons. They are very stable and less
reactive so they are known as paraffin. They are also called alkanes.
The general formula of these compounds is C_H,n +2 where ‘n’ is the

number of carbon atoms. Here n= 1, 2,3,4
