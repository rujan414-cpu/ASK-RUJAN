5.5 Heart attack

In general, the process that
continues in the heart is blood
coming to the heart from various
parts of the body, and blood going
to various organs from the heart.
But sometimes, the flow of blood
to the heart tissues is suddenly
reduced or blocked, and in this
situation, the heart cannot do its
work properly, and the person
feels extreme difficulty. This
condition is called heart attack.
This is a serious and emergency
condition.
