Factors affecting rate of chemical reaction:

1. Catalyst

Activity 15.7

Objective: To observe the effect of a catalyst in the rate of chemical
reaction.

Materials: test tubes, hydrogen peroxide (H,O,), Manganese dioxide
(MnO,)

Procedure:
1. Take two test tubes.

2. Put 5 ml of hydrogen peroxide in each of the test tubes with the
help of your teacher.

3. Put 2g of manganese dioxide (MnO,) in one of the test tubes.

4. In which of the test tube does the chemical reaction complete
faster? Observe and draw a conclusion.

Conclusion:

The substance which increases or decreases the rate of chemical
reaction is called a catalyst. These substances change the rate of
chemical reaction but they do not undergo permanent chemical
changeduring chemical reaction. The catalyst which increases the
rate of reaction is called a positive catalyst. For example, Manganese
dioxide (MnO,), Vanadium Pentoxide (V,O.), iron (Fe),etc. are some
positive catalysts. Similarly, the catalyst which decreases the rate
of chemical reaction is called a negative catalyst. For example,
Phosphoric acid (H,PO,) is a negative catalyst.
