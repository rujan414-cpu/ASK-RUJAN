Artificial greenhouse

Artificial greenhouses are made of
transparent plastic or glass which
allow solar radiation to enter into
them. They are also known as hot
houses. The structure made of glass
and plastic to keep plants is called a
greenhouse. The short waves of solar
radiation can enter the greenhouse
where some of the energy is absorbed
by the greenhouse and they change to
long waves. These waves with long
wavelengths cannot escape from there.
These long wavelength radiations
changes into heat energy. As a result,
heat is stored inside the greenhouse
causing temperature to increase inside
