Coronary angioplasty and stenting

This medical procedure is used to open the clogged coronary artery
of the heart, also called percutaneous coronary intervention (PCI).
During angioplasty, a cardiologist puts a long, thin, flexible tube
called a catheter into a blood vessel of the hand or leg and guides it
to the narrowed artery of the heart. A stent (a small metal mesh tube)
