b. Radius of curvature

The radius of curvature is the distance from the center of curvature
to the surface of the lens. In a ray diagram, it is denoted by R.

c. Optical center

The optical center is the geometrical center of the lens, where the

principal axis intersects the lens surface. In a ray diagram, it is denoted
by O.

Figure 10.54 Terminologies related to lens

d. Principal axis

The principal axis is the line passing through the optical center and
the two centers of curvature of the lens. It divides the lens into two
symmetric halves.
