Amphibia

Activity 2.9 Observation of the frog

Objective: To identify the characteristics of amphibians
Material: Frog

Method

i. Take a frog.

ii. Observe its external structure thoroughly.

iii. With the help of the teacher, identify it's all organs.
iv. Note its characteristics based on your observation.

v. On the basis of observation of the frog, discuss the
characteristics ofamphibia.

Conclusion

Toad Frog Salamander
Fig.2.23

Toads, salamanders, and frogs live on land and water both. They
belong to the class Amphibia. The characteristics of animals in
this class are given below:

a. Their body is covered by moist skin.

b. Their body is divided into head and trunk. Tail is found in
some amphibians.
