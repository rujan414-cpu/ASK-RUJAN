Aldrin,Dieldrin, DDT, BHC, Chlordane, Lindane, Endosulfan, etc.
are the insecticides which are all banned in Nepal. The production,
buying, selling, export, and import of Dichlorvos insecticide has
been restricted in Nepal.

The haphazard use and disposal of industrial chemicals like cement,
ceramics, glass, plastic, fiber, chemical fertilizers, soap, detergent has
degraded the quality of different components of the environment like
air, water, soil, etc. which causes imbalance in the ecosystem. So, this
type of pollutants must be used and managed properly. They should
not be disposed directly in the sources of water and agricultural land.
