The production of motion in the wire placed in a magnetic field when
current is passed through it is called the motor effect. Fans, water
pumps, mixer grinders, etc. work because of the motor effect.

In Figure 11.19, the direction of the magnetic field of a bar magnet
and the circular magnetic field developed around a wire when electric
current flows in it are shown. The force of attraction and repulsion
between the two magnetic fields produces motion in the wire.
