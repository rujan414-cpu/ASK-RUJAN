Preventive measures of blood pressure

High blood pressure leads to various physical and mental problems.
Therefore, high blood pressure can be avoided by paying attention
on time. Following are the ways to avoid blood pressure:

a. Pay attention to the kind of daily meal that you consume.
Consume less salty and oily food items.

b. Avoid smoking and drinking alcohol.

c. Take balanced diet in food.

d. Daily physical exercise is essential.

e. Do yoga, meditation and pranayama.

f. Take precautions by checking blood pressure frequently.

g. Live a stress-free life.
h. Participate in various recreational activities.
Diabetes

Due to various causes, the insulin hormone that helps in the
management and utilization of glucose obtained from our food
becomes low, and blood sugar level rises. A condition in which the
amount of glucose in the blood is more than required is called diabetes.
It is also called hyperglycemia.
