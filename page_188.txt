Free fall

As observed in activity 7.3, a sheet of paper and a falling stone can be
considered as free fall when the air resistance on them is negligible.
In that case, the acceleration of the stone is equal to the acceleration
of gravity (9.8 m/s’) ofthe earth. Thus, an object falling under the
influence of gravity alone without any obstruction is said to be in free
fall. The acceleration of an object in free fall is equal to the acceleration
due to gravity (g).
