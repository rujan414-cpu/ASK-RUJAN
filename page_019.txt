Environment is a home for microscopic organisms to giant
organisms like bluewhale, elephant, banyan, peepal, etc. On
the basis of structure of cell, body structure, mode of nutrition,
presence or absence of cell wall etc., Robert Harding Whittaker
classified these organisms into the five kingdoms. Among them,
we already discussed about kingdoms monera, protista and fungi
in grade nine. Here, lets recall the main characteristics of the

organisms that belong to those three kingdoms.

All the organisms that belong to monera have prokaryotic cell.
Protista includes unicellular organisms whereas fungus consists
of saprotrophic organisms that have cell wall but no chlorophyll.
