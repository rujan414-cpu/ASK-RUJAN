Method

i. Make a model of 22 pairs of somatic chromosomes and the single
paired sex chromosome found in either males or females by using
different coloured clay, after observing the chart or picture of
the human karyotype.

ii. Now, paste these chromosome models serially on the cardboard
or thermocol.

iii. Present the karyotype model that you prepared in class and
discuss the somatic chromosomes and sex chromosomes.

a. Somatic chromosomes

The chromosomes that determine the physical characteristics of
an individual are called somatic chromosomes. In a pair of somatic
chromosomes, each member has the same morphology. So, these
chromosomes are also called autosomes.

b. Sex chromosomes

The chromosomes that determine the sex of an individual are
called sex chromosomes. The structure of each member of a pair
of sex chromosomes is different. So, these chromosomes are called
heterosomes.
