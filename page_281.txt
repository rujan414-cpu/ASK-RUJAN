Power of a lens

As in activities 10.14 and 10.11, take a ray box with three slits and
three concave and convex lenses having different thicknesses. Then
perform the following activities.

a. Allow the light rays from the ray box to fall on the lenses of your
friend's or relative’s spectacles and then observe their converging
and diverging capacities. Then after, ask whether the power of the
lens is -4 dioptre (D) or +2 dioptre (D), and so on, to the owner
of the respective spectacles. Here, the lenses having positive
power are convex lenses whereas those having negative power
are concave lenses. The power of a lens is measured in dioptre.
