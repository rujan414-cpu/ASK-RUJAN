a. Their body is elongated, cylindrical and segmented both

b. The body is bilaterally symmetrical and triploblastic.

f. The circulatory system is well-developed. Haemoglobin is

g. The nervous system consists of a nerve ring and nerve cord

h. The digestive system is well-developed.
i. Some of them are hermaphrodite and some are unisexual.
j. They have a high regeneration capacity.

k. They are found in moist lands, water, and some of them are
ectoparasites.
