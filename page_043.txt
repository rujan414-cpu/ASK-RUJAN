Slug and snail are found in damp soil. Octopus and cuttlefish
are found in marine water. They have tentacles in their head.
Tentacles are sensory in function and are used to detect
surrounding stimuli. They also have muscular feet which are
used for locomotion. So, these animals are grouped under the
phylum Mollusca.
