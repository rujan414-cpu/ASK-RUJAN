Project work 2.3 Study the relationship between classification of
living beings and evolution

Take a classification chart of living beings. Study the basis
of classification in detail. Discuss with your friends the
characteristics of animals of different groups in the chart. Identify
their similarities and differences. Take the help of teacher and
internet, if required. Based on your findings, prepare a short
report on the relation between classification of living beings and
evolution. If possible, prepare a powerpoint presentation of the
report and present it to the class.
