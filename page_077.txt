All living things do not live forever. When organisms become mature,
they produce offspring like themselves. In all organisms, characters are
passed on from the previous generation to the next generation, so the
offspring of all organisms resemble their parents. Chromosomes found
in the nucleus of the organism are responsible for this. During cell
division, chromosomes replicate and divide to pass into daughter cells.

of several other factors, including the environment contributes to the
variation in organisms of the same species. Chromosomes determine
