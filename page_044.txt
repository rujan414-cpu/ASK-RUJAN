Echinodermata

Starfish Sea cucumber Sea urchin
Figure 2.20

Animals shown in the picture are spiny skinned. They belong
to the phylum echinodermata. Following are the characteristics
of animals under this phylum:

a. Their body is externally covered by hard calcareous (made
ofcalcium carbonate) spines.

b. Animals are of various shapes like globular, star-like,
elongated and spherical.

c. They do not have distinct head.

d. They are triploblastic and radially symmetrical animals.
