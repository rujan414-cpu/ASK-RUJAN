Due to human activities and changes in nature in course of
modern development, the existence of animals has decreased.
There is a chance of extinction of many plants and animals. The
main reasons for this are as follow:

a. Due to the adverse effects of weather and climate change,
the distribution pattern of plants and animals is affected

b. Uncontrolled use of biological resources is causing a decline
in their numbers.

c. Growing new species of plants without any research has
affected the indigenous species of plants.

d. Rearing new species of animals without any research has
displaced indigenous or prevailing animals of that area.

e. Environmental pollution is increasing.
