The main purpose of cross breeding is to enhance the quality of the
hybrid. Qualities of the hybrid are not transferred to all subsequent
generations.

Some organisms produced from cross breeding
Liger

The hybrid animal obtained by crossing the male lion and female tiger
is called liger. It is a most known hybrid animal. It is larger than its
parents. It generally behaves like the lion.
