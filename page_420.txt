Minerals

Picture 17.2 potassium, sodium, and calcium rock

Almost all metals, except some, are found in the form of
compounds on the earth’s crust. These elements and compounds
which are found naturally are called minerals. Minerals are the
sources of metals. The rocks found on earth’s surface are mainly
made of metallic and nonmetallic matter. Some rocks are made
of one or more types of minerals. Non-reactive metals like gold,
silver, etc. are found in pure state in nature whereas reactive
metals like sodium, potassium, calcium, iron, etc. are found in
the form of compounds. Almost all minerals are inorganic, pure,
solid and crystalline in nature. In this way, the pure, solid and
crystalline compounds found in nature are called minerals. They
have a fixed chemical formula. The earth’s crust is composed
of about 90% of silicate minerals. Besides this, sulphide, oxide,
carbonate, sulphate, phosphate ores are also found on it. Minerals
like hematite, cuprite, granite, limestone, talc, red clay, coal,
etc. are found in different parts of Nepal. Dang, Salyan, Rolpa,
Gulmi, Pyuthan, Lalitpur, Palpa, Nawalparasi are some places
where minerals are found in Nepal.
