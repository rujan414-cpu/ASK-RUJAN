Figure 9.4 Relation between the volume and density of water, and between the
density and temperature of the water

When most substances are heated, their volume increases. But water
shows a different nature. Although its volume increases when heated,
its volume decreases on heating from 0°C to 4°C. Also, on cooling
down to 4°C, its volume decreases, but from 4°C to 0°C its volume
increases (expands). Therefore, water at 4°C has the minimum volume
and the maximum density.This unique property of water is known
as the anomalous expansion of water.
