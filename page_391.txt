Chemical equation:

During a chemical change, there is loss and gain or sharing of electrons
between the reactants where they either combine or decompose or
exchange their ions to form products. Such a change process is known
as chemical reaction. The chemical reaction in which the reactants
and products are written in the form of words, symbols orchemical
formula is known as chemical equation.
