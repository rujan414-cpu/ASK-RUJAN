Normally, when light falls on the interface between any two media, a
part of it is reflected and the rest is refracted to the other medium. But.
in the condition of total internal reflection, all the light is reflected.
Therefore, the word ‘total’ is used here. The necessary conditions for
the total reflection of light are given below.

a. Light must be travelling from the denser medium to the rarer
medium.

b. The angle of incidence must be greater than the critical angle.
