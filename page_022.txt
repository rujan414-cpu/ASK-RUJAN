Spirogyra Volvox Fucus
Figure 2.4

In plants -Spirogyra, Volvox, Fucus etc. shown in the picture,
root stem and leaf cannot be differentiated. Their plant body
is called thallus. Their cells contain cell wall and chlorophyll.
These plants belong to division algae. Characteristics of the plants
under this division are as follow:

a. They are either unicellular or multicellular.

b. They are autotrophs due to the presence of chlorophyll.
They prepare and store food in the form of starch.

c. Their cell wall is composed of cellulose.

d. They reproduce both sexually and asexually.

e. These plants are found in pond, river, sea and marshy places.
Example: Chlamydomonas, Volvox, Spirogyra, Ulothrix, Fucus etc.
