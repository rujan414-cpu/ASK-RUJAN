Homologous series

The series of hydrocarbons which can be represented by the same
general formula is called a homologous series. Each member of this
series is called a homologue. All the members of the series can be
represented by a common formula and the adjacent homologue of
the series differs by CH2 group. Similarly, the molecular weight of
adjacent members of the series or homologue differs by 14.
