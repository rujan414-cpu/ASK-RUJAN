Chemical pesticides
Activity 19.3

What type of insecticides are being used in the agricultural land and
garden of your locality? observe and search,and then discuss on the
following questions:

a) What are the advantages of using insecticides?

b) What local methods have been used to control pests and insects
in your area?

c) Are there any negative effects of using insecticides?

Let's study a case

According to the news
published on 15th Shrawan
2079 BS, in the ‘The
Kathmandu Post’, a farmer
in Haripur Municipality was
burnt badly at his back due
to the leakage of insecticide,
which he was carrying while
spraying the chemical in his
field. Similarly a study shows that 22 persons had already died of
cancer in Ghurkauli, a main area of growing vegetables in Hariban
Municipality. The number of people suffering from blood and skin
cancer is also more in that area. These are only some examples in
the case of Nepal. In fact, there is an excessive and unscientific
use of pesticides in vegetables, fruits, and agricultural products
in different regions of Nepal.
